We want to remove the separation between Opp Score and Usage Score on the Player Overview tabs and replace them with a single unified composite:

role_score
Goal

For Player Overview datasets, combine the old:

opp_score
usage_score

into one new metric:

role_score

This should represent the player’s total role + opportunity profile in one 0–100 score.

Important architecture change

Going forward, the Player Overview composite stack should be:

role_score
efficiency_score
overall_score

And then the frontend can still compute/display:

rb_tier
wr_tier
te_tier

If opp_score and usage_score still exist in backend temporarily for compatibility, that is okay short-term, but the preferred final UI should not show them.

What Role Score should mean

role_score = a unified measure of:

raw opportunity volume
role strength
weekly deployment quality
premium/scoring-area usage

This should answer:

How strong and fantasy-relevant is this player’s offensive role right now?

It replaces the old “Opp Score” + “Usage Score” split.
