Definitions
targets = total targets
epa_per_target = EPA divided by targets
success_rate = percent of targets producing positive EPA
yards_per_target = receiving yards / targets
catch_rate = receptions / targets
fpoe = fantasy points over expectation
yards_per_route_run = receiving yards / routes run
targets_per_route_run = targets / routes run
air_yards_per_target = air yards / targets
air_yards_share_pct = share of team air yards as decimal
explosive_play_rate = percent of receptions gaining 15+ yards
explosive_rec_20_plus = receptions of 20+ yards
explosive_rec_40_plus = receptions of 40+ yards
longest_reception = longest single reception
yac_per_rec = yards after catch / receptions
ybc_per_rec = yards before catch / receptions
broken_tackles = broken tackles on receptions
btkl_per_rec = broken tackles / receptions
WR Efficiency Score formula

Compute in pipeline as a 0–100 composite from percentile ranks:

EPA / TGT = 25%
YPRR = 25%
Success Rate = 20%
Yds / TGT = 15%
Explosive Play Rate = 10%
BTKL / Rec = 5%
Data rules
WRs only
no duplicate players
percentages stored as decimals
protect divide-by-zero
round rate stats to sensible precision
sort default by wr_efficiency_score descending, then yards_per_route_run descending
